## Target and testable buyer problem

**Best initial target category:** a local owner-operated home-service business (e.g. HVAC, plumbing, painting, landscaping) that wants more customers to **call or request a quote** from a clean, credible mobile website.

**Discovery hypothesis — not a claim about an actual prospect:** some businesses may have an outdated or confusing mobile site, buried click-to-call information, unclear services/service areas, or an inquiry form with no dependable delivery. Validate an individual prospect's real website before mentioning any defect, wasted ad spend or lost leads. No prospect/client named or researched here.

**The customer-facing promise we can safely make:** "Rovor can build a focused mobile-friendly page that explains your services and gives customers a clear way to contact you or request a quote. We'll test the agreed links and form with you before launch." This describes a proposed service and verification process, **not** a quantified result, guaranteed lead count, SEO ranking or conversion uplift.

1. Business name, existing website, service categories and service area; validate publicly observed current site and address any client corrections.
2. Primary outcome: phone calls, quote request, booking, or other specifically selected action. Pick **one** main CTA.
3. Verified phone, inquiry destination, basic form fields, business email and approved privacy/contact consent handling.
4. Approved logo and photos; exact copy about services, years in business, certifications and customer results must be supplied/authorized, not inferred.
5. Existing domain/hosting access, or an agreed supported alternative, plus explicit deployment permissions.
6. Buyer decision maker, acceptance process, scope exclusions, price/payment, deadline/review rounds and who handles updates after delivery.

Until these are known, the scope is an internal proposal; no project is authorized to publish or promise a due date.

## Actual delivery protocol (once a client agrees)

- **Intake:** record needs, source content, permissions and agreed scope in a private authorized place (not public GitHub files). Create a versioned internal intake packet.
- **Build:** create editable source and private preview. Test site navigation, click-to-call, form validation and layout on actual available browsers/devices; record browser, viewport and result.
- **Client review:** send the *specific preview version* to the authorized decision maker. Log corrections. No inference of acceptance from silence.
- **Launch:** publish only to an explicitly authorized domain/hosting target and maintain a known-good rollback version.
- **Verify and hand off:** a **real test inquiry** must reach the client's confirmed destination; obtain confirmation, verify CTA and HTTPS at the live URL, deliver source/access/support terms and log customer approval.

## Measurable outcome for Rovor's first real customer

This offer itself counts as a **reusable sales artifact only**. The **first commercial accomplishment** requires:

- [ ] A named, qualified and verified business with a real contact problem or goal (not a guessed website deficiency).
- [ ] Accepted, versioned scope and actual agreed commercial terms.
- [ ] Working website/page and inquiry behavior tested at the actual agreed destination.
- [ ] Client approval of the exact release and recorded handoff.
- [ ] Invoice/payment evidence before claiming revenue.
- [ ] If measuring conversion improvements: actual time range, baseline, data source and consistent denominator. No invented gain.

**Practical decision:** use this offer to qualify one genuine business, not to build dozens of unsold generic demo sites. The next customer-facing deliverable is an *actual authorized page and verified inquiry*, separate from the MP3-first original animation development.
