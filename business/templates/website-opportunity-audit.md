## Evidence rules

Every finding must be classified as one of:

- **Observed:** directly visible in the inspected page or returned by a named test. Link the exact source and note the page/section.
- **Hypothesis:** a plausible customer-journey consequence of an observed fact. State what would verify or disprove it.
- **Unknown:** cannot be established from available evidence, such as actual lead volume, form delivery, mobile layout, analytics, ownership, budget, or decision-maker.

Never describe an inferred issue as a proven business loss. Do not claim lost calls, low conversion, wasted ad spend, poor search ranking, or a revenue opportunity without reliable data from an authorized source. Do not infer a broken form from its appearance; test submission only with explicit authorization and an agreed test destination.

## Customer-journey checks

For each row, record an evidence URL, observation, and any unknown. Use **U** if the item was not actually verified.

| Dimension | Score | What to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary offer clarity | U / 0 / 1 / 2 | Can a visitor tell what service is offered and who it is for? |
| Service area | U / 0 / 1 / 2 | Is the actual service area clear without guessing? |
| Main contact action | U / 0 / 1 / 2 | Is one relevant next action easy to find and understand? |
| Call/contact details | U / 0 / 1 / 2 | Are visible contact details consistent and useful for the intended audience? |
| Quote/booking path | U / 0 / 1 / 2 | Is the next step understandable, with no observed dead end or unexplained duplicate path? |
| Form burden and clarity | U / 0 / 1 / 2 | Are required fields labelled, justified, and proportionate to the initial inquiry? |
| Trust and factual proof | U / 0 / 1 / 2 | Are claims, credentials, reviews, service photos, and business details clearly sourced and credible? |
| Mobile/accessibility | U / 0 / 1 / 2 | Only score after an actual responsive/accessibility check; otherwise mark U. |
| Content and asset relevance | U / 0 / 1 / 2 | Do headings, images, and service descriptions match the actual service? |
| Privacy and consent clarity | U / 0 / 1 / 2 | Are form purpose, consent, and privacy information clear for the actual data collected? |

Scoring: **0** = a concrete, evidenced gap; **1** = partly clear or inconsistent, with evidence; **2** = appears clear for the checked scope; **U** = not checked or evidence insufficient. Scores describe only the inspected pages and method. Do not turn a score into a predicted conversion rate or revenue.

## Fit and disqualification

- **Fit for Rovor's proposed one-page service-business offer:** strong / possible / weak / unknown
- **Why it fits:** cite evidence about business type, audience, service, and observable website need.
- **Scope that could solve it:** describe the smallest deliverable and one primary CTA.
- **What existing site already does well:** list these explicitly.
- **Disqualifiers:** no credible observable need; the existing flow already meets the stated goal; business is outside the agreed market; unclear rights/permissions; required work exceeds the proposed scope; or no authorized decision-maker/contact route.
- **Unknowns to ask the owner:** desired outcome, actual inquiry destination, current site constraints, approved content/assets, budget, timeline, and who approves the release.
- **Qualification status:** research candidate / needs owner confirmation / qualified after direct confirmation. Public web research alone cannot establish budget, need, authority, or intent.

## Privacy and outreach boundary

- This template is safe for the public repository only when filled with generic examples and public-source facts approved for publication.
- Keep named prospect lists, internal fit scores, outreach plans, private contact notes, client data, and credentials in an authorized private business system—not this public repository.
- Do not submit forms, call, email, message, or publish an offer from this audit. Outreach requires a separate explicit decision and verified recipient.
- Do not buy lead lists, scrape private data, fabricate testimonials, promise leads or ROI, or imply a website problem caused lost business without evidence.
