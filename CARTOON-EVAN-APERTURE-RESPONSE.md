# Direct inner/outer lip response experiment

The previous painted construction still had a large release step. Its inherited SolidCavityHead.oral_target multiplied the already changing speech aperture by another smoothstep opening gate. Below an aperture of 8px that gate compressed the whole oral drawing; above it the full outer drawing was exposed. At source frames 740–742, this produced outer control heights of 2.2000, 19.3546 and 29.2391px. These are control measurements, not measured visible lip velocity or proof of acoustic sync.

ApertureResponseCartoonHead is a new opt-in experiment. It keeps the existing voice, estimated phones, weights, mouth width/corners, source textures, skin and independent chin controls. It replaces only the non-F/V oral vertical mapping. The inner opening follows max(.15, aperture−1) and the outer lip follows that opening plus a 2.2px drawn rim allowance. Each loop normalizes its existing vertical shape around the shared corner chord. This removes the second whole-mouth opening gate and keeps separate inner/outer lip extents. It is an authored cartoon response, not measured speaker biomechanics, recovered lip motion or artist-verified anatomical correspondence. F/V pure and mixed weights retain the previous profile exactly. No source images were generated or edited, and no normal scene/default renderer was changed.

The thinner rim exposed an existing occlusion error: the previous interior mask used the filled outer cavity silhouette. This experiment clips retained teeth/tongue to the actual 16-point inner-lip contour, sampled at 3x and retracted by one native pixel behind the ink. Contact fully excludes interior surfaces. Antialiased edge pixels can straddle the analytic boundary; half-covered or stronger pixels stay inside it. This is geometry-based occlusion, not painting over a tooth artifact.

The acoustic review tool plots the original waveform, 5ms RMS and a 256-sample/32-hop spectrogram around “small”, alongside the existing estimated phone labels and reviewed 48fps frames. There is voiced low-frequency energy within part of the estimated M interval, followed by stronger vowel energy; RMS alone cannot locate a consonant boundary. No labels were moved. Exact boundaries remain unverified, including the earlier 18 flagged regions. This is visual evidence only, without independent listening acceptance.

Four tests check positive oral triangles through pure shapes and 1,000 deterministic mixtures, preservation of all X coordinates/corners/non-oral controls and input immutability, absence of a second threshold jump under a fixed-shape aperture sweep, exact F/V mixed-weight retention, and inner-lip containment/zero interior coverage at contact. These are mechanical checks, not a claim of professional animation quality.
