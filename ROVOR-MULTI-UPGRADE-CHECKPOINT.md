## Work completed in five distinct areas

1. **Actual-voice Moho switch timing (`evan-engine/voice_scene_adapter.py`).** Converts existing, user-authorized V3 `scene.json` Rhubarb X/A–H mouth cues to one-based 24/25/30/48/60fps `MohoSwitch1` `.dat` frames. Samples the middle of each output frame, deduplicates repeated poses, validates contiguous cue topology, keeps decoded WAV SHA-256 and duration, and explicitly identifies unverified phoneme boundaries. Actual recovered reference WAV run: **22.0589375 s, 16 kHz PCM16 mono**, **142 cues -> 142 24fps switch keys**, **4 subframe cues** requiring caution. Source `scene.json` declares 22.047375 seconds, the ~11.56ms difference is within the accepted tolerance. These are historical V3 cues, **not** proven accurate for the later updated Evan phoneme-plan master. The **18 later flagged words remain unreviewed**. No silent correction.
2. **Editorially authored multi-shot plan (`evan-engine/production_shot_plan.py`).** Typed, no-gap 24fps/30fps/48fps storyboard plan with shot purposes, independently timed acting beats, 16:9 and 9:16 safe-zone metadata, style and art-approval fail-closed gates, and optional rover cameo grounded in specific source IDs. **No loudness-driven camera shaking or emotion inference**. A draft *favicon* story follows recovered scene beats as browser problem -> favicon detail -> clear brand payoff -> Rovor signoff. Frames **530**, four shots, **one-frame decoded-audio tail padding**, none production approved. This is a proposed animatic contract, not an animated clip.
3. **Quality-check dailies (`evan-engine/motion_dailies_qa.py`).** Examines real rendered PNG frame sequences for true-black frames, large unexplained frame jumps and extended near-identical frame runs; declared cuts are respected. Applied to the previous *rejected* 96-frame rough SVG footage: no black frames or unexplained jumps, but **three >=12-frame near-identical runs**, with median frame difference ~0.000167 at diagnostic downsampled resolution. Such tests cannot determine aesthetic appeal; they are one additional triage signal, not an acceptance score. **No old rough render should be presented as a new preview.**
4. **Authentic rover source integrity (`evan-engine/rover_reference_gate.py` plus `evan-character/rover-reference.manifest.json`).** The two user-supplied, not model-invented rover reference PNGs are verified by SHA-256 and pixel dimensions, with visual intent documented: compact autonomous white R-profile enclosed rover, dark glass and lower band, 4 wheels, sensor pod and side logo; **not a rugged off-road truck, SUV or ordinary pickup**.
